Essential Italy Tips 意大利旅行必备贴士

🎫

Book Museums Early提前订博物馆

The Uffizi, Accademia and Colosseum all sell out weeks ahead. Book before you fly.

乌菲齐、学院美术馆和斗兽场常在数周前售罄，请在出发前预订。

🚆

Take the Train乘坐火车

High-speed trains connect major cities in under 2 hours. Faster and cheaper than flying.

高速列车连接主要城市，2小时内即可到达，比飞行更快更便宜。

🕐

Go Early or Late早去或晚去

Crowds peak 10am–4pm. The Trevi Fountain at 7am and the Colosseum at 5pm are different experiences.

人潮高峰在10点至16点。清晨7点的特莱维喷泉和17点的斗兽场，体验完全不同。

💶

Eat Like a Local像当地人一样用餐

Stand at the bar for coffee (half the price). Avoid restaurants with photos on the menu.

站在吧台喝咖啡（价格减半）。避开菜单上有图片的餐厅。
