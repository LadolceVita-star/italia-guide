The Amalfi Coast is one of those places that genuinely earns its reputation. Cliffs plunge 400 metres into turquoise water. Pastel villages cling to the rock face. Lemon groves perfume every hillside road. It is not a place you pass through — it is a place you rearrange your schedule to linger in.

The key to doing it well is transport planning. The SS163 coastal road is spectacular and terrifying in equal measure. Knowing when to take the ferry, when to take the bus, and when to simply walk the clifftop paths separates a frustrating trip from an unforgettable one.

阿马尔菲海岸是那种完全配得上它盛名的地方。悬崖从400米高处直插绿松石色的海面，粉彩色调的村庄紧贴岩壁而建，柠檬树林让每一条山路都充满香气。这里不是一个过路的地方，而是一个让你心甘情愿调整行程、多停留几天的地方。

玩好阿马尔菲的关键在于交通规划。沿海公路SS163既壮观又惊险。知道什么时候坐渡船、什么时候坐巴士、什么时候沿着悬崖步道步行，是好旅程与糟糕旅程的分水岭。

Top Attractions 主要景点

01

Positano

The postcard village. Stacked pink and white houses cascading to a pebble beach. Arrive before 9am or after 5pm to see it without the crowds. The beach (Spiaggia Grande) charges a sun-bed fee — walk left for the free section.

明信片上的村庄。粉白相间的房屋层层叠叠延伸至鹅卵石海滩。9点前或17点后到达，才能避开人潮。大海滩收取躺椅费，向左走可找到免费沙滩区域。

02

Ravello

High above the coast (350m), Ravello is cooler, quieter, and more elegant. The Villa Cimbrone gardens offer the most famous view on the entire coast — the Terrace of Infinity. Wagner composed here. Book the gardens early.

位于海岸350米之上，拉韦洛更凉爽、更宁静、也更优雅。奇布罗内别墅花园拥有整条海岸最著名的景色——无限露台。瓦格纳曾在此创作。建议提前预订入园时间。

03

Amalfi Town & Cathedral 阿马尔菲镇与大教堂

The town that gave the coast its name. The Arab-Norman cathedral (€3) is genuinely stunning. The medieval paper museum (Museo della Carta) tells the story of how this small republic once traded paper with the Arab world. Allow half a day.

赋予海岸名字的城镇。阿拉伯-诺曼式大教堂（€3）令人叹为观止。中世纪造纸博物馆讲述了这个小共和国曾与阿拉伯世界进行纸张贸易的历史。留出半天时间慢慢游览。

04

Path of the Gods (Sentiero degli Dei) 众神之路

Italy's most dramatic coastal hike. 7.8km from Agerola to Nocelle, mostly descending, with the Tyrrhenian Sea 400m below you the entire way. Take the early SITA bus to Agerola, walk to Nocelle, descend the 1,700 steps into Positano. Allow 4–5 hours.

意大利最壮观的海岸徒步路线。从阿杰罗拉到诺塞勒7.8公里，大部分为下坡，全程俯瞰400米之下的第勒尼安海。乘坐早班SITA巴士到阿杰罗拉，步行至诺塞勒后沿1700级台阶下降至波西塔诺。预计耗时4–5小时。

4-Day Amalfi Coast Itinerary 阿马尔菲4日行程

Day 1 · 第一天

Arrive in Positano — Settle & Explore 抵达波西塔诺，安顿与初探

Take the SITA bus from Naples or Salerno. Check in, then walk the village lanes in the late afternoon light. Dinner in the backstreets — avoid the waterfront restaurants (overpriced). Order fresh pasta with clams and a carafe of local white wine.

从那不勒斯或萨勒诺乘坐SITA巴士前往。办理入住后，在傍晚的余晖中漫步村中小巷。在小巷里用餐，避开海滨餐厅（价格虚高）。点一份新鲜蛤蜊意面和一壶当地白葡萄酒。

Day 2 · 第二天

Path of the Gods Hike 众神之路徒步

Early SITA bus to Agerola (buy the day pass). Hike Sentiero degli Dei to Nocelle. Descend the steps into Positano. Afternoon swim and gelato. This is the best day on the coast — do not skip it for a beach day.

早班SITA巴士前往阿杰罗拉（购买全日票）。徒步众神之路至诺塞勒，然后沿台阶下降至波西塔诺。下午游泳、吃冰淇淋。这是海岸最值得体验的一天，不要为了海滩日而跳过这段旅程。

Day 3 · 第三天

Amalfi Town & Ravello 阿马尔菲镇与拉韦洛

Morning ferry to Amalfi. Visit the cathedral and the paper museum. After lunch, take the bus up to Ravello. Walk to Villa Cimbrone for the Terrace of Infinity. Stay for sunset — the light on the water is extraordinary from 350 metres up.

早晨乘渡轮前往阿马尔菲镇。参观大教堂和造纸博物馆。午餐后，乘巴士前往拉韦洛。步行至奇布罗内别墅观赏无限露台。留下来看日落——从350米高处俯瞰海面上的光线令人叹为观止。

Day 4 · 第四天

Boat Trip & Emerald Grotto 游船出行与翡翠洞

Rent a small boat from Amalfi harbour or join a shared boat tour (book via GetYourGuide the night before). Visit the Emerald Grotto (Grotta dello Smeraldo), swim in the hidden coves accessible only by sea, and see the coast from the water — which is the only way to fully understand its scale.

从阿马尔菲港口租一艘小船或参加共享游船（前一晚通过GetYourGuide预订）。游览翡翠洞（Grotta dello Smeraldo），在只能从海上抵达的隐秘海湾游泳，从海上欣赏海岸——这是真正理解其壮观规模的唯一方式。

🚌 Transport Reality Check / 交通实况 The coastal road gets gridlocked in July and August. A 10km journey can take 2 hours by bus. Use the ferry whenever possible between Positano, Amalfi, and Salerno — it's faster, cheaper, and far more pleasant. The SITA bus pass is €3.50 for a single journey or €12 for a day pass. 7、8月份沿海公路经常严重堵车，10公里的路程坐巴士可能需要2小时。尽量在波西塔诺、阿马尔菲和萨勒诺之间乘坐渡轮——更快、更便宜、也更舒适。SITA巴士单程票€3.50，全日票€12。

Food & Drink 美食与饮品

The Amalfi Coast kitchen is built on three things: lemons, seafood, and buffalo mozzarella from the nearby Paestum plains. The lemon here (Sfusato Amalfitano) is larger, sweeter, and more fragrant than any other in Italy — and it goes into everything.

阿马尔菲海岸的饮食文化建立在三样东西上：柠檬、海鲜，以及来自附近帕埃斯图姆平原的水牛马苏里拉奶酪。当地柠檬（Sfusato Amalfitano）比意大利其他任何地方的柠檬都更大、更甜、香气更浓——几乎融入每一道菜。

Scialatielli ai Frutti di Mare

Local thick pasta with mixed seafood. The definitive dish.

当地宽面配什锦海鲜，是这里最具代表性的一道菜。

Limoncello

Made with Sfusato Amalfitano lemons. Drink it ice-cold after dinner.

用当地柠檬制成，饭后冰镇享用风味最佳。

Mozzarella di Bufala

From Paestum, 40 minutes south. Eat it alone with good olive oil and salt.

来自南部40分钟车程的帕埃斯图姆，直接搭配好橄榄油和盐食用即可。

Delizia al Limone

The coast's signature dessert — a dome of sponge filled with lemon cream.

海岸的招牌甜点——海绵蛋糕包裹柠檬奶油馅，外形如圆顶。

Practical Tips 实用小贴士

  • Book hotels 3–6 months ahead for July and August. The coast has limited accommodation and prices triple in peak season.
  • 7月和8月的酒店需提前3–6个月预订。海岸住宿有限，旺季价格可涨至3倍。
  • Wear proper walking shoes, not flip-flops. The path of the gods and most village steps require grip.
  • 穿好徒步鞋，不要穿人字拖。众神之路及大多数村庄台阶需要防滑鞋底。
  • The ferry timetable changes seasonally. Always check travelmar.it for current schedules before you plan your day.
  • 渡轮时刻表按季节调整。每天出行前务必在 travelmar.it 查询最新班次。
  • Avoid driving if you can. The SS163 is one-lane in many sections and parking is nearly impossible in Positano.
  • 尽量避免自驾。SS163公路许多路段仅单车道，波西塔诺几乎找不到停车位。
