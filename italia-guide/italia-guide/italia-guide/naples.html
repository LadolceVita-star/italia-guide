Naples is Italy at its most raw, most beautiful, and most chaotic. It is the only city in Europe where you can eat the world's greatest pizza for €5, walk through 2,000-year-old underground tunnels, and watch Mount Vesuvius loom over the bay — all before lunch. It is also Italy's best base for day trips: Pompeii, Herculaneum, the Amalfi Coast, and Capri are all within 90 minutes.

那不勒斯是意大利最原始、最美丽、也最混乱的城市。它是欧洲唯一一个你可以花€5吃到世界上最美味的披萨、穿越2000年历史的地下隧道、并在午餐前就能看到维苏威火山耸立在海湾上的城市。它也是意大利最佳的一日游基地：庞贝、赫库兰尼姆、阿马尔菲海岸和卡普里岛都在90分钟车程之内。

Top Attractions主要景点

01

Pompeii & Herculaneum

The ancient Roman city buried by Vesuvius in 79 AD. Pompeii is larger and more famous; Herculaneum is smaller but better preserved — the organic materials (wood, food) survived. Take the Circumvesuviana train from Naples (35 minutes). Book skip-the-line tickets online.

公元79年被维苏威火山掩埋的古罗马城市。庞贝更大更著名；赫库兰尼姆较小但保存更完好——有机材料（木材、食物）得以留存。从那不勒斯乘坐环维苏威火车（35分钟）前往。建议网上购买免排队门票。

02

Naples Historic Centre

A UNESCO World Heritage Site — the most intact ancient city street plan in the world. Walk the Spaccanapoli (the arrow-straight street that splits the city), explore the underground Naples (Napoli Sotterranea) and visit the Cappella Sansevero for the extraordinary Veiled Christ sculpture.

联合国教科文组织世界遗产——世界上保存最完整的古代城市街道布局。漫步斯帕卡那波利（将城市一分为二的笔直街道），探索地下那不勒斯，参观圣塞韦罗礼拜堂，欣赏非凡的蒙面基督雕像。

03

Capri Day Trip

45 minutes by hydrofoil from Naples. The Blue Grotto (Grotta Azzurra) is the main attraction — a sea cave where the water glows electric blue. Visit early morning to avoid coach tour crowds. Anacapri, at the top of the island, is quieter and equally beautiful.

从那不勒斯乘水翼船45分钟可达。蓝洞（Grotta Azzurra）是主要景点——海水在洞中发出电蓝色光芒。早晨前往可避开旅游团人群。岛上高处的阿纳卡普里更为宁静，风景同样美丽。

04

Street Food & Markets

Naples is Italy's street food capital. The Mercato di Porta Nolana is the city's best fish market. Via dei Tribunali is lined with the city's best pizzerias. Budget €5–8 for a margherita at a local pizzeria — this is some of the best value eating in Europe.

那不勒斯是意大利的街头美食之都。诺拉纳门市场是城市最好的鱼市。特里布纳利大道两侧排列着城市最好的披萨店。在当地披萨店点一份玛格丽特披萨只需€5–8——这是欧洲性价比最高的美食之一。

3-Day Naples Itinerary那不勒斯3日行程

Day 1 · 第一天

Historic Centre, Pizza & Underground Naples历史中心、披萨与地下那不勒斯

Morning: walk Spaccanapoli from east to west, stopping at the Cappella Sansevero (Veiled Christ — book ahead, only 100 visitors at a time). Lunch: pizza at L'Antica Pizzeria da Michele or Sorbillo — arrive before noon or expect a wait. Afternoon: Napoli Sotterranea (underground Naples tour). Evening: walk along the seafront promenade (Lungomare).

上午：从东到西漫步斯帕卡那波利，在圣塞韦罗礼拜堂停留（蒙面基督——需提前预订，每次仅限100人）。午餐：在L'Antica Pizzeria da Michele或Sorbillo吃披萨——中午前到达，否则需要等待。下午：那不勒斯地下城市导览。傍晚：沿海滨长廊（Lungomare）散步。

Day 2 · 第二天

Pompeii Day Trip庞贝一日游

Take the 7:40am Circumvesuviana train to Pompeii Scavi. Arrive before the coach tours. Spend 4–5 hours exploring — the Forum, the Lupanare, the Villa of the Mysteries, and the Garden of the Fugitives are essential. Return to Naples by 3pm. Evening: dinner in the Quartieri Spagnoli (Spanish Quarter) — the real neighbourhood restaurants.

乘坐7:40早班环维苏威火车前往庞贝考古遗址。在旅游团到来前抵达。用4–5小时探索——广场、妓院、神秘别墅和逃亡者花园是必看之处。下午3点前返回那不勒斯。晚上：在西班牙区用餐——那里才是真正的街区餐厅。

🍕 Pizza Rule / 披萨规则 Neapolitan pizza is protected by law (STG certification). It must be made with specific flour, San Marzano tomatoes, and Fior di Latte mozzarella. The best pizzerias have a queue — this is a good sign. Never order a pizza in a restaurant that also serves pasta, hamburgers, and sushi. 那不勒斯披萨受法律保护（STG认证）。必须使用特定面粉、圣马扎诺番茄和菲奥尔迪拉泰马苏里拉奶酪制作。最好的披萨店门口有队伍——这是好兆头。永远不要在同时供应意面、汉堡和寿司的餐厅点披萨。
Day 3 · 第三天

Capri or Amalfi Coast卡普里岛或阿马尔菲海岸

Choose your day trip. Capri: take the 9am hydrofoil (book tickets the night before at the port). Amalfi Coast: take the SITA bus from Naples to Positano (2 hours) — or a faster ferry in summer. Both are spectacular. Capri is more glamorous; Amalfi is more dramatic.

选择你的一日游目的地。卡普里岛：乘坐9点水翼船（前一晚在港口购票）。阿马尔菲海岸：从那不勒斯乘坐SITA巴士前往波西塔诺（2小时）——夏季可选择更快的渡轮。两处都令人叹为观止。卡普里更为精致华丽；阿马尔菲更为壮观震撼。

Food & Drink in Naples那不勒斯美食与饮品

Naples is arguably Italy's most important food city. Pizza was invented here. So was pasta al ragù, sfogliatella, and baba. The street food culture is extraordinary — eating while walking is not just accepted, it's encouraged.

那不勒斯可以说是意大利最重要的美食城市。披萨在这里诞生。肉酱意面、千层酥和朗姆蛋糕也是如此。这里的街头美食文化令人叹为观止——边走边吃不仅被接受，更是被鼓励的。

Neapolitan Pizza

Margherita or Marinara. Soft, charred, eaten immediately.

玛格丽特或水手披萨。柔软、带焦痕，出炉即食。

Sfogliatella

Naples' iconic shell-shaped pastry, eaten warm from the oven.

那不勒斯标志性贝壳形酥皮点心，趁热从烤箱取出即食。

Limoncello

Made with Amalfi lemons. Served ice-cold after any meal.

用阿马尔菲柠檬酿制，餐后冰镇享用。

Pastiera Napoletana

Traditional Easter tart with ricotta and candied fruit.

传统复活节塔，内含乳清奶酪和蜜饯水果。
