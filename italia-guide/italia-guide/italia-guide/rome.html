Vatican, Colosseum

Rome is the only city in the world where you can eat a cornetto for breakfast in the shadow of a 2,000-year-old temple. The layers of history here are genuinely overwhelming — ancient ruins sit beside Renaissance palaces, which sit beside Baroque fountains. The trick to doing Rome well is accepting that you cannot see everything, and choosing your priorities ruthlessly.

罗马是世界上唯一一个你可以在2000年前的神庙阴影下享用早餐可颂的城市。这里的历史层次令人叹为观止——古代遗迹紧邻文艺复兴宫殿，文艺复兴宫殿旁又是巴洛克喷泉。玩好罗马的诀窍在于接受你无法看遍一切，然后无情地选择重点。

Top Attractions主要景点

01

Colosseum & Roman Forum

The world's greatest amphitheatre. Book tickets at least 2 weeks ahead — queues without a ticket can exceed 3 hours. Combine with the Roman Forum and Palatine Hill (included in the same ticket).

世界上最伟大的圆形剧场。至少提前2周购票——没有门票的排队时间可能超过3小时。门票包含罗马广场和帕拉蒂尼山，建议一并游览。

02

Vatican Museums & Sistine Chapel

Michelangelo's Sistine Chapel ceiling is one of the great achievements of human art. Book the earliest possible slot — the chapel gets extremely crowded by 11am. The museums require 3–4 hours minimum.

米开朗基罗的西斯廷礼拜堂穹顶是人类艺术的伟大成就之一。预订最早的场次——礼拜堂在11点前就会极度拥挤。博物馆至少需要3–4小时。

03

Trevi Fountain & Spanish Steps

The Trevi Fountain at 7am — before the crowds — is one of Rome's great experiences. The Spanish Steps are best at dusk. Both are free. Neither requires booking.

清晨7点的许愿池——在人群到来之前——是罗马最美好的体验之一。西班牙台阶在黄昏时分最为迷人。两处均免费入场，无需预订。

04

The Pantheon

Built in 125 AD and still the best-preserved ancient building in the world. Entry is now €5 (timed tickets required at coopculture.it). Go early — the oculus light effect is extraordinary in morning sun.

建于公元125年，至今仍是世界上保存最完好的古代建筑。现收费€5，需在coopculture.it购买定时票。建议早去——穹顶圆孔的光影效果在晨光中令人叹为观止。

4-Day Rome Itinerary罗马4日行程

Day 1 · 第一天

Ancient Rome — Colosseum, Forum & Palatine古罗马——斗兽场、广场与帕拉蒂尼山

Start early at the Colosseum with your pre-booked ticket. Walk through the Roman Forum and up to Palatine Hill. Afternoon: the Circus Maximus and a walk along the Tiber. Evening in Trastevere — Rome's most atmospheric neighbourhood for dinner.

凭预购门票，早早前往斗兽场。穿越罗马广场，登上帕拉蒂尼山。下午：马克西穆斯竞技场和台伯河漫步。晚上前往特拉斯提弗列——罗马最有氛围的晚餐街区。

Day 2 · 第二天

Vatican Day — Museums, Sistine Chapel & St Peter's梵蒂冈日——博物馆、西斯廷礼拜堂与圣彼得大教堂

Full day at the Vatican. Start at the museums with your earliest possible slot. Sistine Chapel. Then St Peter's Basilica (free, no booking needed) and climb the dome (€8) for the best view over Rome. Cross Castel Sant'Angelo on the way back.

全天游览梵蒂冈。从博物馆最早场次开始。西斯廷礼拜堂。然后前往圣彼得大教堂（免费，无需预订），登上穹顶（€8）俯瞰罗马全景。返程途中经过圣天使城堡。

💡 Insider Tip / 内行建议 The Vatican Gardens tour (booked separately) gives you access to the Sistine Chapel from behind — entering before the crowds. It's more expensive but worth it if you want the chapel to yourself for 10 minutes. 梵蒂冈花园游览（单独预订）可让你从后方进入西斯廷礼拜堂——在人群到来之前入场。价格较贵，但如果你想独享礼拜堂10分钟，绝对值得。
Day 3 · 第三天

Centro Storico — Pantheon, Piazzas & Fountains历史中心——万神殿、广场与喷泉

7am at the Trevi Fountain (empty and magical). Breakfast at a bar standing up. Morning at the Pantheon (book timed ticket). Piazza Navona for lunch. Afternoon at Campo de' Fiori market. Aperitivo at sunset on a rooftop bar near the Pantheon.

清晨7点在许愿池（空旷而神奇）。站在吧台享用早餐。上午游览万神殿（需购买定时票）。在纳沃纳广场午餐。下午逛鲜花广场市场。日落时分在万神殿附近的屋顶酒吧享用餐前酒。

Day 4 · 第四天

Borghese Gallery, Trastevere & Farewell Dinner博尔盖塞美术馆、特拉斯提弗列与告别晚餐

Morning at the Borghese Gallery — Bernini sculptures that will stop you in your tracks. Book 2 weeks ahead (entry strictly timed, 2 hours only). Afternoon: walk through Trastevere and explore the backstreets. Final dinner: cacio e pepe at a neighbourhood trattoria.

上午游览博尔盖塞美术馆——贝尼尼的雕塑会让你驻足凝视。需提前2周预订（严格限时，仅2小时）。下午漫步特拉斯提弗列，探索小巷。告别晚餐：在街区小餐馆品尝奶酪黑胡椒意面。

Food & Drink in Rome罗马美食与饮品

Roman cuisine is defined by simplicity and quality ingredients. The four classic pasta dishes — cacio e pepe, carbonara, amatriciana, gricia — are all Roman inventions. Do not add cream to carbonara. You will regret it.

罗马料理以简洁和优质食材著称。四大经典意面——奶酪黑胡椒、卡邦纳拉、阿马特里恰纳、格里恰——都是罗马的发明。不要在卡邦纳拉里加奶油，你会后悔的。

Cacio e Pepe

Pecorino, black pepper, pasta. Rome's most iconic dish.

佩科里诺奶酪、黑胡椒、意面。罗马最具代表性的菜肴。

Carbonara

Egg yolk, guanciale, Pecorino. No cream, ever.

蛋黄、猪颊肉、佩科里诺奶酪。永远不加奶油。

Supplì

Roman fried rice balls with mozzarella. Eat them from street vendors.

罗马炸饭团，内含马苏里拉奶酪，在街边小摊品尝最佳。

Artisan Gelato

Avoid places with mountains of brightly coloured gelato — go for the flat, covered trays.

避开颜色鲜艳、堆得像山的冰淇淋店，选择放在平盘并加盖的手工冰淇淋。

Practical Tips实用贴士

  • Dress code is strict at the Vatican and major churches — shoulders and knees must be covered. Carry a scarf.
  • 梵蒂冈和主要教堂对着装要求严格——肩膀和膝盖必须遮盖。随身携带一条围巾。
  • July and August are brutally hot (35°C+). Start sightseeing before 9am and take a long lunch break.
  • 7月和8月酷热难耐（35°C以上）。9点前开始游览，并享用悠长的午餐休息时间。
  • The metro is limited — Rome is best explored on foot or by taxi. Avoid tourist buses.
  • 地铁线路有限——步行或打车是游览罗马的最佳方式。避免乘坐旅游巴士。
  • Tap water is safe and delicious in Rome. Refill your bottle at the free "nasoni" street fountains.
  • 罗马的自来水安全可口。可在免费的"nasoni"街头饮水喷泉处补充饮水。
