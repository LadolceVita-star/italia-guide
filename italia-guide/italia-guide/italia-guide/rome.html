Milan is Italy's most misunderstood city. Most visitors treat it as a gateway — a place to fly into before heading south. This is a mistake. Milan is a sophisticated, design-obsessed metropolis with world-class museums, a cathedral that took 600 years to build, and one of the greatest paintings in human history hidden in a refectory wall. It rewards those who stay.

米兰是意大利最容易被误解的城市。大多数游客把它当作一个门户——飞抵后再向南出发的中转站。这是一个错误。米兰是一座充满设计感的精致大都市，拥有世界级博物馆、一座耗时600年建造的大教堂，以及隐藏在餐厅墙壁上的人类历史上最伟大的画作之一。它值得你留下来细细品味。

Top Attractions主要景点

01

Milan Duomo & Rooftop

The largest Gothic cathedral in the world, built over 600 years. The cathedral is free; the rooftop (€13) gives you an extraordinary close-up of the 3,400 statues and spires. Book the rooftop online to avoid queues.

世界上最大的哥特式大教堂，历时600年建造。大教堂免费参观；屋顶（€13）让你近距离欣赏3400座雕像和尖塔。建议网上提前预订屋顶门票以避免排队。

02

The Last Supper (Cenacolo)

Leonardo da Vinci's masterpiece, painted 1495–1498. Only 30 visitors are admitted every 15 minutes. Tickets sell out months ahead — book at vivaticket.com the moment you plan your trip. This is non-negotiable.

达·芬奇的杰作，创作于1495–1498年。每15分钟仅允许30名游客入内参观。门票提前数月售罄——制定行程时立刻在vivaticket.com购票。这是必做之事，没有妥协的余地。

03

Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II

Italy's oldest shopping mall, built in 1877 — a stunning iron-and-glass arcade connecting the Duomo to La Scala opera house. Walk through it at least twice: once during the day, once at night when it's illuminated.

意大利最古老的购物拱廊，建于1877年——一座华丽的铁与玻璃拱廊，连接大教堂与斯卡拉歌剧院。至少走两遍：白天一次，夜晚灯火通明时再走一次。

04

Brera & Navigli Districts

Brera is Milan's most charming neighbourhood — art galleries, independent boutiques, and the excellent Pinacoteca di Brera art gallery. Navigli (the canal district) is where Milan does aperitivo — go at 6pm for the best atmosphere.

布雷拉是米兰最迷人的街区——艺术画廊、独立精品店和卓越的布雷拉美术馆。纳维利（运河区）是米兰人享用餐前酒的地方——傍晚6点前往，体验最佳氛围。

3-Day Milan Itinerary米兰3日行程

Day 1 · 第一天

Duomo, Galleria & Brera大教堂、拱廊与布雷拉

Start at the Duomo — arrive early to beat the school groups. Climb to the rooftop. Walk through the Galleria to Piazza della Scala. Afternoon in Brera: the Pinacoteca (Raphael's Betrothal of the Virgin is here) and the neighbourhood streets. Evening aperitivo in Navigli.

从大教堂开始——早到以避开学生团体。登上屋顶。穿过拱廊走向斯卡拉广场。下午游览布雷拉：布雷拉美术馆（拉斐尔的《圣母的婚礼》就在这里）和周边街道。傍晚在纳维利享用餐前酒。

Day 2 · 第二天

The Last Supper & Fashion District最后的晚餐与时尚区

Morning: The Last Supper (at your pre-booked time — arrive 15 minutes early, no late entry). The experience lasts exactly 15 minutes. Afternoon: walk the Quadrilatero della Moda (Golden Rectangle) — Via Montenapoleone, Via della Spiga. Window shopping is free. Evening: dinner in Isola neighbourhood.

上午：最后的晚餐（按预订时间前往——提前15分钟到达，不接受迟到入场）。整个体验精确持续15分钟。下午：漫步时尚四边形（黄金四边形）——蒙特拿破仑大街、斯皮加街。橱窗购物免费。晚上：在伊索拉街区用餐。

💡 Last Supper Booking / 最后的晚餐预订 Book at vivaticket.com exactly 3 months before your visit — tickets are released in batches and sell out within minutes. Set a calendar reminder. If you miss the official tickets, some tour operators offer "unofficial" viewings — these are legitimate but more expensive. 在参观日期前整整3个月登录vivaticket.com购票——门票分批发售，几分钟内售罄。设置日历提醒。如果错过官方票，部分旅行社提供"非官方"参观——这些是合法的，但价格更贵。
Day 3 · 第三天

Lake Como Day Trip科莫湖一日游

Take the 40-minute train from Milan Centrale to Como. Hop on the ferry to Bellagio — the most beautiful village on the lake. Walk the promenade, visit Villa Melzi gardens, and have lunch overlooking the water. Return train from Como in the late afternoon. Back in Milan by 7pm.

从米兰中央火车站乘坐40分钟火车前往科莫。搭乘渡轮前往贝拉吉奥——湖上最美丽的村庄。漫步长廊，参观梅尔齐别墅花园，在湖景餐厅享用午餐。傍晚从科莫乘火车返回，19点前抵达米兰。

Food & Drink in Milan米兰美食与饮品

Milan's cuisine is richer and more northern than Rome or Naples — butter replaces olive oil, risotto replaces pasta. The city also invented aperitivo culture: at 6pm, order a Campari Spritz and get free snacks.

米兰的饮食比罗马或那不勒斯更为丰富、更具北方特色——黄油取代橄榄油，烩饭取代意面。这座城市还发明了餐前酒文化：傍晚6点，点一杯金巴利气泡酒，免费小吃随之而来。

Risotto alla Milanese

Saffron risotto. The defining dish of Milan.

番红花烩饭。米兰最具代表性的菜肴。

Cotoletta alla Milanese

Milan's breaded veal cutlet — the original schnitzel.

米兰炸小牛排——原版维也纳炸肉排。

Aperitivo Hour

€10 buys a Spritz and access to a free buffet. Milan invented this.

€10享有一杯气泡酒加免费小吃自助。这是米兰的发明。

Panettone

Milan's famous Christmas bread — but sold year-round in the best pasticcerie.

米兰著名的圣诞面包——但在最好的糕点店全年有售。
